Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

We believe in transparent clinical education. Here are clear answers to common hair transplant queries.

Is an FUE hair transplant permanent?

+
Yes, hair transplants are permanent. Grafts are harvested from the occipital region (back and sides of the scalp), which is genetic-resistant to Dihydrotestosterone (DHT), the hormone responsible for male pattern baldness. Once implanted, they continue to grow for life.

Does the hair transplant procedure hurt?

+
No, the procedure is virtually painless. We utilize high-quality local anesthesia on both the donor and recipient areas. You are awake throughout the day, and can comfortably watch movies, use your phone, or chat with our clinical staff.

How many grafts will I need for full coverage?

+
This depends entirely on the extent of your thinning/baldness (classified via the Norwood Scale). Typical cases require between 2,500 to 4,500 grafts. A precise estimation is given during our free scalp analysis and consultation.

What is the recovery time and downtime?

+
Initial micro-crusts form over 3-5 days. Most patients return to desk jobs within 4 days. Redness and minor swelling fade after 7 to 10 days, after which your transplant becomes practically unnoticeable.

What is "shock loss" and is it normal?

+
"Shock loss" is a temporary shedding of the transplanted hair shafts, which typically occurs between weeks 3 to 8. This is a completely normal biological reaction. The follicles remain active under the skin and start producing permanent, thick hair from month 3 onwards.

What is the difference between FUE and DHI?

+
In FUE, the surgeon manually creates micro-channels in the skin prior to inserting grafts. In DHI, extraction is identical, but graft insertion uses a specialized Choi Implanter Pen that does channel creation and implantation simultaneously, allowing for maximum density and faster recovery.
