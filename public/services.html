FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) Hair Transplant

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) represents the gold standard in modern surgical hair restoration. Individual hair follicles are harvested with micro-precision from the donor area (back of the head) and densely packed into the bald or thinning areas. Since each follicle is handled separately, there are no linear scars, and recovery is incredibly swift.

Key Benefits of FUE:

  • ✓ 100% natural, lifelong hair growth results.
  • ✓ No linear surgical scalpel incisions, meaning minimal healing time.
  • ✓ Virtually painless procedure under specialized local anesthesia.
  • ✓ Donor area heals fully within 5 to 7 days.
  • ✓ Maximum follicle survival rates exceeding 95%.

Ideal Candidate: Men and women suffering from androgenetic alopecia, temporal hairline recession, or Crown-area thinning who possess solid donor hair density on the back of the scalp.

DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) Premium

Direct Hair Implantation (DHI) is an advanced evolution of the FUE method. Using patented Choi Implanter Pens, follicles are extracted and directly implanted into the scalp without the need for pre-creating channel incisions. This provides our surgeons with extreme control over the growth direction, angle, and depth of every single hair graft, delivering unmatched natural density.

Key Benefits of DHI:

  • ✓ Simultaneous channel creation and graft insertion for rapid results.
  • ✓ Reduced time of grafts remaining outside the body, ensuring maximum survival.
  • ✓ Ability to perform "Shave-Free" transplants for busy executives.
  • ✓ Superior control over hair angle for designing ultra-refined frontal lines.
  • ✓ Faster healing with practically zero post-op swelling.

Ideal Candidate: Patients wanting elite-density hairline restoration or those who prefer not to shave their head for the surgical procedure.

Exosome Hair Regeneration Therapy

Exosome therapy represents the absolute cutting-edge of non-surgical biorestorations. Exosomes are cellular micro-vesicles containing highly concentrated signaling proteins, growth factors, and mRNA. When injected into thinning areas of the scalp, they activate dormant stem cells and trigger weak, miniaturized hair follicles to transition back into the active growth (anagen) phase.

Key Benefits of Exosomes:

  • ✓ Up to 100x more growth signals compared to traditional PRP.
  • ✓ Markedly slows down active genetic shedding within weeks.
  • ✓ Thicken existing miniaturized hairs, boosting global head volume.
  • ✓ Accelerates post-surgical graft healing and growth speeds.
  • ✓ Completely non-surgical, painless, and completed in 30 minutes.

Ideal Candidate: Individuals experiencing early diffuse hair thinning, female pattern hair loss, or post-surgical patients looking to maximize graft growth.

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Hair Therapy

PRP is an organic, autologous therapy that relies on the natural healing powers of your own blood cells. We draw a small sample of blood and spin it in a premium medical centrifuge to isolate the Platelet-Rich Plasma. This gold-colored liquid, rich in fundamental growth factors, is then gently micro-injected into your scalp to stimulate blood flow and nourish starved roots.

Key Benefits of Hair PRP:

  • ✓ Natural treatment using exclusively your own plasma—no foreign chemicals.
  • ✓ Revitalizes blood circulation to weak hair follicles.
  • ✓ Promotes noticeable thickness and controls excessive daily hair fall.
  • ✓ Safe and clinically proven with zero risk of allergic reactions.
  • ✓ Highly cost-effective hair maintenance option.

Ideal Candidate: Men or women noticing initial hair thinning, weak hair texture, or those wishing to maintain overall scalp health naturally.

Painless Laser Hair Removal

Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art triple-wavelength laser systems that selectively target melanin in hair roots. By destroying the root's nutrient supply without affecting the surrounding skin, we provide highly permanent hair reduction. Our integrated sapphire cooling tips ensure a comfortable, painless experience.

Key Benefits of Laser Removal:
